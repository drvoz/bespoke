01

One craft, four boundaries

Every offering comes from the same craft: designing an experience so that people leave seeing, deciding, or working differently. What changes is the boundary being crossed. Each one opens into a deeper dive.

OFFERING 01 · LEAD TOPIC Human ↔ machine

What Must Stay Human

Creativity, judgment, and ownership in the age of AI. Most AI talks ask what the technology can do. This one asks what leaders must deliberately keep human, and how to know whether they have.

Deep dive → OFFERING 02 Knowing ↔ doing

Designing Experience That Changes Behavior

What two decades of immersive simulation, serious games, and behavioral engineering teach about how capable adults actually learn and change, and how to design for it in any organization.

Deep dive → OFFERING 03 Place ↔ behavior

Space as a Leadership Tool

How the design of physical and experiential territory shapes collaboration, learning, and safety. Space is something you can rehearse and test before anyone depends on it.

Deep dive → OFFERING 04 Those who do ↔ those who discover

Crossing the Blood–Brain Barrier

A tested method for building partnerships between operations and research: matching needs to skills and data, turning hunches into hypotheses, and agreeing on outcomes both sides value.

Where it started · 2008

“The hardest thing in that room wasn't the science. It was the bridge.”

On the national consensus conference that shaped the work

In 2008, as co-chair of a federally funded national consensus conference on the science of simulation, John discovered that building bridges between people who know different things is a skill. It is not a byproduct. That conference produced a journal issue. It also set the course of a career.

03

Formats

Bespoke is the default. Each format is designed around the audience, the program's goals, and the time available.

45–90 minutes

Keynote

A story-driven talk with live audience moments and a closing discussion.

Half or full day

Experiential workshop

Participants do the thing, then debrief it. Built on simulation-style design and structured reflection.

Cohort or one-to-one

Partnership design session

Facilitated sessions that match needs, skills, and data, and leave with a shared hypothesis and measures.

1–3 days · Peoria, Illinois

Immersive visit

A hosted program at Jump Trading Simulation & Education Center, including hands-on simulation.

Multi-session

Program module

A module designed with host faculty and woven into an executive or international learning program.
